Pricing

Atox is free with the subscription you already have. Or pick a plan and get AI models right inside Atox, no separate accounts or API keys.

Questions

How is usage counted?

Every request to a model uses tokens, and bigger models use your plan faster than smaller ones. Pro gives you 3× and Max 5× the usage of Start. Cheaper models go further: the same plan gives you many more requests with DeepSeek or Qwen than with Claude Sonnet.

What happens when I run out?

Atox models pause until your plan renews. You can upgrade at any time, and you can always keep working with your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription for free.

Does unused usage carry over?

No. Included usage resets at the start of every billing period.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel in the app and your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. See the refund policy for refunds.

Do I need a plan to use Atox?

No. Atox is free and open source. Plans are only for people who want AI models without a separate Claude or ChatGPT subscription.

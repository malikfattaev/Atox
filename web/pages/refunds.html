Refund Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The Atox app is free. This policy covers Atox paid plans (Start, Pro and Max). Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record, and refunds are issued through them to the original payment method.

Cancelling

You can cancel your subscription at any time in the Atox app. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

First purchase: 14-day refund

If you are not happy with your first purchase of a plan, ask for a refund within 14 days of the payment. You get a full refund if you have used less than 25% of the usage included in that period.

Renewals

Renewal payments are not refundable once the new period has started, because the included model usage is paid to model providers as you use it. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. If you forgot to cancel and have not used the new period at all, write to us within 7 days of the renewal and we will refund it.

Problems on our side

If Atox models were unavailable for a significant part of your billing period because of a problem on our side, or you were charged by mistake, we refund the affected amount in full.

How to ask for a refund

Write to malikfattaevx@gmail.com from the email of your Atox account, or reply to your Dodo Payments receipt. We answer within 3 business days. Approved refunds usually reach your account within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

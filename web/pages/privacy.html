Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Atox is operated by Abdumalik Fattaev, an individual based in Uzbekistan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data Atox collects and why. In short: your projects, files and chats stay on your computer unless you send them to an AI model yourself.

Data that stays on your computer

Your projects, chats, settings and drafts are stored locally on your computer. We never receive them. Agents you run with your own subscription talk to their provider (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) directly, under that provider's privacy policy.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics to Aptabase so we know how many people use Atox: an install event once, then one event per day, week and month the app is open. Each event carries only the app version, operating system name and version, and locale. No identifiers, projects, files, chats or IP addresses are stored. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy.

Update checks

The app checks GitHub for new versions. GitHub sees a regular web request, as with any download.

Paid plans

If you buy an Atox plan, we also process:

  • Account data: your email address and the state of your subscription, to sign you in and give you access to your plan.
  • Usage records: which model was used, how many tokens and what it cost, to apply your plan's limits. We do not store the content of your prompts or of model responses.
  • Requests to models: when you use an Atox model, your request passes through our server to the model provider, through OpenRouter, only to get the response back to you. Providers process it under their own privacy policies.
  • Payments: handled by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record. Your card details go to Dodo Payments; we never see them. We receive the order and subscription status.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. We share it only with the services above, as needed to run Atox, or when the law requires it.

Retention and your rights

We keep account data and usage records while your account exists and as long as accounting rules require after that. You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete your account by writing to the address below.
